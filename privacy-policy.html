Privacy Policy — Polarity

Last updated: 05 September 2026. Developer: Normann Games. Contact: Normann_games@proton.me

Polarity is a single-player arcade game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of our own. This policy explains the little data that is involved and who handles it.

What the game itself stores

Your best scores, energy shards, purchased items, unlocked achievements and settings are saved on your device only, in the app's local storage. This information is never transmitted to us and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it. The in-game backup code exists so you can copy your progress yourself — it is generated on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Advertising

Polarity shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To do this, the AdMob SDK collects and shares certain data:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)Selecting and measuring ads, limiting repeats, detecting fraud
Ad interactions (impressions, clicks)Measuring ad performance
Coarse device and app informationDelivering ads that work on your device

Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent form before any personalised advertising, and may choose non-personalised ads or withdraw consent later from the game's Options screen.

If you purchase the No Ads product, interstitial advertising stops. Rewarded video remains available only if you choose to watch it.

Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. Google's handling of that data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What we do not collect

No name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location, microphone or camera access. The app requests no runtime permissions beyond network access, which is used only for ads and purchases.

Children

Polarity is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data through this app, contact us at the address above and we will act on it.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to export or erase on request. To remove everything associated with the app, reset your Advertising ID and uninstall the app. For questions about data held by Google, use the links above. If you are in the EEA or UK you may also contact your national data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date, and the app listing will be updated.
