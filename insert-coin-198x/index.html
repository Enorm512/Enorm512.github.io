INSERT COIN 198X

Two buttons. Forty years.

It starts as a forgotten 1983 cabinet: two colours, a black sky, a ship that moves left and right and shoots. Then the hardware upgrades while you are still playing, and keeps upgrading, through 8-bit, 16-bit, early 3D, neon bullet hell and beyond. The controls never change. Everything else does.

Play in your browser Free. No download, no account. Desktop keyboard or gamepad; touch works too.

A playable history of the arcade

1983 · 1988 · 1992 · 1997 · 2005 · 2015 · 2025 · 20XX

Eight generations, each with its own look, its own soundtrack and its own boss. The same small red alien you meet in the first wave comes back later as a glowing interceptor, because the whole universe evolves with the machine. Beat the final boss and the cabinet reboots into a second, faster loop.

Controls

←→ move
SPACE fire
SHIFT overdrive: clears every bullet

That is all of it. A player who understands the game in the first ten seconds still understands it in the final stage. The difficulty comes from the enemies, the bosses and the scoring, never from the buttons.

Chase the score

  • ChainsKill quickly to raise a multiplier up to ×100. The cap grows with each era.
  • GrazingLet shots brush past the ship for points and extra chain time.
  • Eight bossesA claw-armed alien, a serpent, a rotating station, a carrier with destructible hangars, a hive, a boss that studies how you play, and one that shifts between graphics generations mid-fight.
  • Power-upsDual cannon, spread, laser, missiles, shield and a drone companion from 1992 onward.
  • SoundtrackOne theme, re-orchestrated for every era: beeps, chiptune, FM, synthwave, glitch.
